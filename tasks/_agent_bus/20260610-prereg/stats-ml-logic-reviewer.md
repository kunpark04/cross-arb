# Pre-freeze audit — allocation pre-registration (2026-06-10)

Status check: `research/allocation-prereg-2026-06-10.md` and `decisions/0014` are **untracked** (verified `git status`), so this audit is genuinely pre-binding. All three CRITICALs are fixable with text/clauses before freeze; none requires abandoning the design.

## Verdict

**Partially sound.** The prereg gets the big things right — genuinely frozen decision constants with pre-epoch provenance, a post-epoch-only dataset matching the `load()` quarantine, deterministic fold boundaries, a lever decomposition, a conjunctive (type-I-conservative) gate set, and an explicit on-failure re-prereg discipline. But as written it would not deliver a valid confirmatory bit, for three reasons: (C1) the **primary inference gate — a per-position bootstrap CI on ΔPnL — is statistically ill-posed** (positions are bankroll-coupled and cluster-correlated, the two policies fund *different* sets so ΔPnL has no per-position pairing, and PnL is a deterministic booked-edge function of the funded set, so resampling positions resamples nothing in the data-generating process; "capital-weighted mean across folds" is additionally undefined); (C2) the **fold design lets one persistent market be funded in multiple folds** (econ/sports markets span event-dates; `one_per_market` dedupes only within the episode set each fold is handed), which double-counts a single real-world opportunity in the pooled estimate, breaks the "K independent replications" claim, and constructs a concrete false-pass path that evades the top-1 *position* share gate; and (C3) **several referenced procedures do not exist as written** — H1's "descending booked-edge order at each capital-availability instant" is not the policy the +9…+141% antecedent tested (code's `reservation` is arrival-order with *permanent* capital-skips), per-category caps and H2 ordering are unimplemented, and `capital_velocity.py` does **not** measure lock-days (they are hardcoded estimates), so the H2 "measured then frozen" step is vacuous as referenced — leaving implementation-time analyst freedom that is itself a tuning channel. Secondary but material: at the 14-day trigger (K=4 folds) the design is **arithmetically incapable** of a distribution-free 95% confirmation at the fold level (exact sign-flip min two-sided p = 0.125; the ⌈2K/3⌉ sign gate passes 31% of the time under a zero-effect null), so the ≥21-day "target" should be the trigger. Every fix below is a sentence or a clause; the most important five are C1, C2, C3, the 21-day trigger, and the verdict-arm/tie/direction decidability pins.

## Findings

### CRITICAL

**C1 — The primary CI gate (per-position bootstrap on ΔPnL) is invalid and underspecified; the pooled estimand is undefined.**
- **Issue.** §3.5 makes "per-position bootstrap (B=10,000, resample funded positions within fold) on ΔPnL = policy − FIFO" with a "capital-weighted mean across folds" the primary §4 pass gate. Four defects: (i) **no pairing** — H1 and FIFO fund *different* position sets, so "ΔPnL" is a difference of two set-sums, not a per-position quantity; resampling "funded positions" (of which arm?) is not defined for a difference of sets. (ii) **Not iid** — positions within a fold are negatively dependent by construction (shared bankroll: `afford = cash // cost_per`, funding one excludes the next, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:124-129`) and positively dependent through event clusters (same city-date buckets, same game). (iii) **No stochastic PnL** — a position's "profit" is the deterministic booked edge at entry (`profit = size * per`, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:134-137`; `per = max(0, avg_edge − haircut − vh)`, lines 49-54). The only randomness in a fold's PnL is *which candidates arrived*; resampling funded positions resamples the algorithm's output, not the data-generating process. (iv) **Undefined pooling** — every fold has the identical fresh $500 (§3.4), so "capital-weighted" is either vacuous (equal weights) or means deployed-capital weights, which are **policy-controlled outcomes** — an analyst-freedom channel on the headline number.
- **Evidence.** Prereg §3.5, §4 bullet 1; `alloc_policy_experiment.py:118-146` (deterministic booked accounting, differing funded sets, bankroll coupling); genealogy: the prior audit (`tasks/_agent_bus/20260610-0522/stats-ml-logic-reviewer.md`, rec #1) proposed the per-position bootstrap as a *concentration/fragility check on a single policy's PnL* — the prereg promoted it to the primary confirmatory gate on a policy *difference*, a role it cannot support.
- **Minimal fix (prereg text).** Replace §3.5's inference clause with: *"Primary inference is at the fold level: ΔPnL_k = PnL_policy,k − PnL_FIFO,k for each fold k; pooled estimate = the unweighted mean of ΔPnL_k (each fold is an identical fresh-$500, 3-day replication); 95% CI = t-interval on the K fold deltas, reported alongside the exact sign-flip permutation p-value. The per-position resample and leave-one-position-out remain REPORTED as concentration diagnostics only (per the 0522 audit's original intent) and are not pass/fail evidence."* Strike "capital-weighted."

**C2 — Fold assignment is underspecified for markets whose event-date is far from their arrival window, and the same market can be funded in multiple folds — a concrete false-pass path.**
- **Issue.** §3.3 folds on "3-event-day blocks (boundary at the event-date partition)". The partition key is the market's **event date** (settlement date in the slug, decision 0009) — but econ markets arrive **weeks before** their event date and sports books open days early. Two failure modes: (a) *assignment ambiguity* — at a June-24 run, the mirror will hold partitions like `transitions-2026-07-02.jsonl` (it already does today, 2026-06-10, verified by listing `Kalshi/data/cross-arb/`): are those June-observed candidates in a fold (which?), or silently excluded (gutting econ — H2's motivating category)? (b) *cross-fold market duplication* — an econ or pre-game sports market re-detects across many days; if folds are run by filtering episodes per block, `one_per_market` dedupes only **within** each fold's episode subset (`run_policy` applies it to the episodes it is handed, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:93-94`), so the same market is a fresh fundable candidate in every fold it touches. One persistent fat market funded in 3 of 4 folds then: passes the sign count, drives the pooled CI, and **evades the ≤33% top-1 position share** (each fold's instance is a separate "position") — precisely the L18/L19 single-observation trap this prereg exists to prevent. Also internally inconsistent: "K = floor(days/3)" vs "final partial block <2 days is dropped" disagree at 14 days (a 2-day remainder is not <2 days — kept as a 5th fold, or dropped per floor()?).
- **Evidence.** Prereg §3.3; data dir listing (future-dated partitions exist today); `analyze_persistence.py:52-92` (`load()` reads all partitions; episodes built globally); `alloc_policy_experiment.py:93-94`; the 0.81d experiment itself funded `urc-…-2026-07-02` (event date ~3 weeks past the window); prior audit INFO(3) flagged the future-partition/span question.
- **Minimal fix (prereg text).** *"Folds are contiguous blocks of UTC ARRIVAL dates (date of open_t); the event-date partition is storage layout only. Each market enters exactly ONE fold — the fold containing its first post-epoch τ-clearing capturable arrival; its later episodes are not candidates in any other fold (extends one_per_market/C8 across folds). K = floor(arrival-days/3); the remainder is always dropped [or: a ≥2-day remainder is kept as a short fold and K = number of blocks — choose one]. Additionally, no single MARKET may contribute >33% of pooled ΔPnL (market-level analogue of the top-1 gate)."*

**C3 — The prereg freezes constants but not the machinery: H1's semantics differ from the antecedent's tested policy, per-category caps / H2 ordering / lock-day measurement / per-pair friction do not exist in the referenced scripts, and there is no code-freeze clause.**
- **Issue.** (a) H1 says "fund in **descending booked-edge order at each capital-availability instant (arrival-or-recycle)**". The validated 0012 machinery is the `reservation` policy: **arrival-order** consideration with a τ gate, and a candidate skipped for insufficient cash is **permanently lost** — never reconsidered when capital recycles (`_order_candidates` reservation = chronological, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:64-66`; skip-and-continue at lines 127-130). The prereg's instant-priority-queue semantics are a *different, stronger, untested* policy, and they are underspecified: when a recycle instant funds a still-open episode mid-life, at what edge and depth (open_net/open_c2 frozen at open, or current)? (b) Per-category caps (weather 20% / sports 10% / econ 5%) cannot be expressed — `run_policy` takes one scalar `max_clip` (line 80-81). (c) H2's ranking policy does not exist in `_order_candidates` (lines 57-77). (d) §3.1 says lock-days are "measured by `capital_velocity.py` on the post-0013 dataset" — but `capital_velocity.py` **does not measure lock-days**: `LOCKUP_PASSIVE = {"weather": 1.2, "sports": 15.0, "econ": 21.0}` are hardcoded estimates (lines 27-28; its own caveat line 91: "lockup-days are estimates"); only *edges* are measured from data (`edges_by_cat`). Re-running it "on the post-epoch data" re-emits the same constants — the freeze step as referenced is vacuous, and any real measurement would be **new, unfrozen code** whose definitional choices (release = pmus endDate? Kalshi settlement? first-of?) are post-data analyst freedom. (e) The friction arm's "per-pair friction haircut" exceeds the machinery: `haircut` is one scalar applied to all candidates (`_profit_per`, lines 49-54). (f) Nothing else exists either: no fold loop, no bootstrap, no pooling code. Writing all of this *after* the data is visible is a tuning channel through implementation choices (tie-breaks, reconsideration rules, measurement definitions) — the exact channel pre-registration is supposed to close.
- **Evidence.** File:line cites above; prereg §1 (H1 text), §1 H2 bullet, §3.1-2; 0012/0013-corrected tables are all `reservation`-semantics results (`allocation-policy-2026-06-10.md`).
- **Minimal fix (prereg text).** Three clauses: *(1) "H1 funding semantics = the 0012-tested rule: candidates considered in arrival order; fund iff booked edge ≥ τ and the category cap permits; a candidate unaffordable at its arrival is not reconsidered."* (This is the minimal-delta-from-validated-code option. If the instant-descending-queue is genuinely wanted instead, specify it fully: eligibility = episode open interval; funding always at open_net/open_c2 frozen at open; deterministic market-id tie-break — and acknowledge it is a new policy without 0012 support.) *(2) "CODE FREEZE: all machinery this prereg requires (per-category caps, H2 ordering, fold harness, fold-level inference, friction formula) is implemented, selftested, and committed BEFORE the run trigger; analysis-code changes after the trigger are treated identically to constant changes."* *(3) "expected_lock_days are frozen NOW: weather 1.2 d, sports 15.0 d, econ = per-market days-to-release (calendar arithmetic, exogenous). Any post-epoch measured update is a labelled sensitivity arm whose measurement procedure ships inside the code freeze."*

### WARN

**W1 — H2 lock-days "measured then frozen" is a same-data estimation; the benign-iff conditions are not stated.**
- **Issue/analysis (audit question A).** Measuring lock-days on the same post-epoch dataset the test runs on is technically a leak, but of the benign **input-measurement** kind, not outcome tuning — *provided* the discipline is explicit. It is benign iff: (i) computed by a pre-pinned deterministic formula on pinned code; (ii) frozen before ANY arm's allocation PnL is computed (§3's step order does say this — credit); (iii) never revisited after any PnL is seen; (iv) a sensitivity arm vs the priors is reported. Only (ii) is currently stated. The channel is narrow regardless — lock_days enters only H2's *ranking key* and cannot touch H1−FIFO. (And per C3(d), the referenced measurement doesn't exist anyway.)
- **Minimal fix.** Adopt C3 fix (3) (freeze priors now), which eliminates the leak entirely; else add conditions (i), (iii), (iv) verbatim.

**W2 — The fold design is structurally biased against H2: booked-at-entry PnL + 3-day bankroll resets truncate exactly the opportunity cost H2 exists to price.**
- **Issue (audit question B, differential-bias half).** Unsettled positions are credited their **full booked profit** at fold end (`total_pnl = realized + unrealized`, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:140-146`) and the bankroll resets each fold — so capital locked *beyond* the fold is never charged. In a 3-day fold, sports (~15 d) and econ (weeks) never recycle under ANY policy; weather recycles ~once. H1 booking a fat slow econ edge pays no lock penalty; H2 skipping it for a fast thin weather arb harvests at most ~1.8 days of redeployment. H2−H1 ≈ 0 **by construction**; the §4 "H2 adopted only if it beats H1" gate will near-automatically fail, and the record would wrongly read "velocity-awareness doesn't help."
- **Minimal fix.** Add to §4: *"An H2 null in 3-day folds is uninformative about deployment-horizon velocity effects and must not be quoted as falsifying velocity-ordering."* Add to §3: a **descriptive** continuous full-span single-bankroll arm (H1 vs H2, PnL per deployed-dollar-day) so the velocity question gets a non-truncated look.

**W3 — The estimand is booked-edge capture, not realized dollars; "CONFIRMED" risks overreach.**
- **Issue.** No position can ever lose: `profit = size * max(0, …)` ≥ 0; settlement outcomes (void events, CLI revisions, naked legs, actual prints) never enter — only expected-cost haircuts. The 0012 econ position booked its "edge" 3 weeks before any print existed. "H1 CONFIRMED" as written reads like validated money.
- **Minimal fix.** One sentence in §4: *"CONFIRMED means H1 captures more BOOKED edge than FIFO under the frozen expected-cost economics — an allocation-efficiency claim, not realized PnL; settlement-realization reconciliation (settle_recon) is a separate open item."*

**W4 — The run date is analyst-chosen in [14 d, ∞) after a descriptive peek — an optional-stopping channel.**
- **Issue.** §2: trigger "≥14 full post-epoch event-days … target ≥21 if accumulation continues", plus a 7-day descriptive peek. Arrival/edge descriptives correlate with ΔPnL potential; choosing *when* to run after seeing them biases the test even with a frozen rule.
- **Minimal fix.** *"The confirmatory run executes on the first daily pull after 21 post-epoch event-days (after 14 only if collection halts before 21); the 7-day peek may not alter timing."* (Also resolves half of W6.)

**W5 — §4 pass/fail is not fully decidable as written (audit question D).** Each is a one-clause fix:
- (a) **Verdict bankroll unstated** — $500 vs $2,000 ("report both", §1): if they disagree, narrative freedom. Fix: *"$500 is the verdict arm; $2,000 is descriptive robustness, with the pre-stated expectation that the effect attenuates as the bankroll unbinds."*
- (b) **CI direction unstated** — "CI excludes 0" is satisfied by a significantly *negative* ΔPnL. Fix: "CI entirely above 0."
- (c) **Ties in the fold sign count** — small folds make ΔPnL = 0 exactly (both arms fund the same set) quite likely; at K=4 needing 3, one tie decides. Fix: *"|ΔPnL_k| ≤ $0.01 counts as a non-win"* (or exclude-and-reduce-K — pick one).
- (d) **Phantom adjudication has no verdict variant** — "reported in BOTH included/excluded variants" (§4) is good, but if they straddle the gate, which decides? Fix: *"If the frozen checklist concludes phantom, the EXCLUDED variant is the verdict (included reported for audit); if adjudication is ambiguous, the position stays IN."*
- (e) **Pooling weights** — covered by C1.
- (f) **Remainder-block contradiction** — covered by C2.
- (g) **Top-1 share underspecified** — which arm (presumably H1)? And the *pooled* share mechanically shrinks ~1/K as folds accumulate, so the gate weakens exactly as data grows. Fix: specify "H1 arm", keep the per-fold report (already in §3.5), and rely on the C2 market-level share as the binding version.
- (h) **The bug-fix door** — "pipeline bug fixes are allowed but force a full re-run" can become fix-until-pass. Fix: *"A bug = divergence from documented intended behavior demonstrated by a failing selftest written BEFORE the fix; the post-fix run is the verdict regardless of direction."*
- Note: requiring floor-only to also beat FIFO is **coherent** with H1's mechanism claim (0012: the floor is the return lever, the cap is risk control) and rightly makes "H1 wins but not via the floor" a FAIL; it does compound power (two CIs must clear), priced into W6.

**W6 — Power at the 14-day trigger is arithmetically inadequate, and the prereg lacks an asymmetry-of-evidence statement for a null (audit question E).**
- **Computed facts** (verifiable): the ⌈2K/3⌉ sign gate passes a zero-effect null **31.25%** of the time at K=4 (3-of-4), and is non-monotone in K (K=6 → 34.4% is *weaker* than K=5 → 18.75%). At fold-level inference (the valid unit per C1), the exact sign-flip permutation's minimum two-sided p is 2/2^K: **0.125 at K=4** — a distribution-free 95% confirmation is *impossible* until K≥6 (p=0.031); K=7 gives 0.016. A t-interval sketch under the effect's known event-driven structure (fold ΔPnL is large only when a FIFO-monopolizing deep arb arrives in that fold → CV plausibly 0.7–1.5): t = √K/CV = 1.3–2.9 at K=4, all below t_crit(3)=3.18; at K=7, passes for CV ≤ 1.0. Context: ~19 τ-clearing arbs / 0.81 d (~23/day, one active pre-0013 cluster — likely an upper bound now that the econ phantoms are quarantined) vs the ≥30-candidate floor ≈ 2.1/day over 14 d: the floor guards against data catastrophe, not power. And the +9…+141% antecedent is the **best-of-4** OOS cap sweep (winner's curse) on 0.4 d — expect the true effect at or below the low end.
- **Minimal fix.** Make ≥21 event-days / K≥7 the trigger (merges with W4). Add: *"A FAIL is 'not confirmed at this n', not 'the floor does not work'; any null result is reported with the realized minimum detectable effect (the smallest pooled ΔPnL the K-fold CI could have excluded at the observed dispersion)."*

**W7 — The friction-arm formula is underspecified and partially unimplemented.**
- **Issue.** §3.2: "per-pair friction haircut = P(leg-fail at the measured median execution latency, from step 1's curve) × naked-leg loss expectation (existing `clip_threshold_test` friction machinery)". Three gaps: the machinery is a **single scalar** haircut applied to all candidates (`run_policy(…, haircut, …)` → `_profit_per`, `alloc_policy_experiment.py:49-54`) — "per-pair" doesn't exist; **"naked-leg loss expectation" is computed nowhere** (`shadow_fill.py` yields leg-fail fractions and survivor edges, lines 115-152; `adverse_selection.py` attributes which venue moved, no dollar loss model); the "measured median execution latency" source is unstated (latency_probe RTT? one-way? which venue/percentile?).
- **Minimal fix.** Pin it: *"One global scalar haircut = fill_fail(L*) × E_loss, where L* = median RTT from latency_probe over the collection window rounded to the nearest shadow_fill L-grid point, fill_fail from the post-epoch shadow_fill curve, and E_loss = [explicit frozen formula — e.g., 0.5 × per-contract cost, or the px-based adverse-move estimate, named and frozen]; applied via run_policy's haircut argument."* Any per-category refinement goes inside the C3 code freeze.

## Audit questions — direct answers

- **A (frozen constants / H2 leak):** §1 constants: genuinely frozen, pre-epoch provenance (I1). H2 lock-days: a same-data **input-measurement** leak — benign iff deterministic pre-pinned formula + frozen before any arm's PnL + never iterated + prior-based sensitivity arm (W1); the channel only reaches H2's ranking key, never H1−FIFO. But the referenced measurement is vacuous — `capital_velocity.py` lock-days are hardcoded (C3d) — so freeze the priors now and the question dissolves. The friction constants have the same structure (W7) plus an unimplemented formula.
- **B (fold design / lock leakage):** No PnL-attribution bias from truncation per se — unsettled positions carry full booked profit symmetrically (`alloc_policy_experiment.py:140-146`). The real defects: same-market multi-fold duplication breaks fold independence and double-counts opportunities (C2 — the binding cross-fold leakage channel), and the booked-PnL + fresh-bankroll design is velocity-blind, biasing H2 to the null by construction (W2).
- **C (bootstrap validity):** Per-position resampling is not defensible for a CI on ΔPnL (C1: bankroll coupling, cluster correlation, deterministic PnL, unpaired sets). Minimal fix: fold-level t-interval + exact sign-flip as primary; per-position/LOO demoted to concentration diagnostics; the §3.5 event-cluster (city-date/game) concentration report is the right companion and should stay.
- **D (decidability):** Not decidable as written — verdict bankroll, CI direction, ties, phantom-verdict variant, pooling weights, remainder rule, top-1 arm (W5 a–h, C1, C2). The floor-only-must-also-beat-FIFO clause **is** coherent with H1's mechanism claim and correctly makes "H1 wins but not via the floor" a FAIL; it costs power (two CIs), which is acceptable and priced into W6.
- **E (power):** Underpowered at 14 d/K=4 by arithmetic, not opinion: sign gate 31% null-pass; fold-level distribution-free 95% impossible below K=6; t-sketch fails at all plausible CVs at K=4 and passes at K=7 for CV ≤ 1 (W6). Trigger at ≥21 event-days; add the null-asymmetry + MDE sentence so a FAIL is interpretable.
- **F (bans vs needs):** "No dropping positions" is compatible with the phantom path only once the verdict variant is pre-declared (W5d). "No re-cutting folds" collides with bug-fix re-runs that change episode reconstruction — the dual-report covers it, add selftest-first (W5h). Channels the prereg currently *allows* that re-open tuning: run-date freedom after a peek (W4), $2k-arm cherry-pick (W5a), "capital-weighted" pooling (C1), and — largest — writing all the missing machinery after the data is visible (C3).

## Sound as written (explicit)

§2's epoch definition, source, and censoring spec (verified against code); the §3 upstream-constants-first step ordering as a discipline; deterministic fold boundaries (no split-point shopping, once C2's assignment rule is pinned); the conjunctive gate structure (I3); the supersession of the 0012 cap sweep; the on-failure re-prereg clause; and the τ booked↔touch arithmetic. The decision to pre-register at all is the correct response to L19 and the two-phantom history.

3. **C3** — pin H1 semantics to the tested rule (or fully specify the new one), code-freeze clause, freeze lock-day priors now: closes implementation-time tuning.
4. **W6 + W4** — 21-day/K≥7 trigger, pinned run date, MDE/asymmetry sentence: makes both PASS and FAIL interpretable.
5. **W5 a–h** — one decidability clause each.
6. **W2** — H2 asymmetry statement + descriptive continuous arm: prevents a false "velocity doesn't help" entry in the project record.
7. **W3, W7, I1, I5** — estimand sentence; friction formula pin; constant enumeration + commit hash; floor scoping.
