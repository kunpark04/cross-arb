## The one real finding: Kalshi maker fees are CATEGORY-SHAPED

Live `fee_type` split of our 23 series (`fee_multiplier=1` everywhere):

| `quadratic` — maker pays **$0** | `quadratic_with_maker_fees` — maker pays 0.0175 |
|---|---|
| **All 5 weather** (KXHIGHTSFO/LAX/NY/MIA/CHI) | MLB, WNBA, NBA, NHL game series |
| 4 esports (CS2/LOL/VALORANT/COD) | ATP, WTA |
| ITF ×2, UFC | **All econ** (CPIYOY, U3, PAYROLLS, GDP, FEDDECISION) |

The maker-fee flag sits exactly on the deep/efficient books; the thin categories are taker-only.
Consequences:

1. `kfee(taker=False)` **overstates** maker cost by the full `0.0175·N·P(1−P)` on weather/esports/
   ITF/UFC. No live impact today (detection + booking are all-taker; `taker=False` appears only in
   ledger self-tests) — documented in `ledger.py`; a per-series `fee_type` model belongs to the
   maker study, its first consumer.
2. **It strengthens the queued maker-side execution study** (todo): a resting Kalshi weather leg pays
   ZERO fee, and a resting pmus leg *earns* −0.0125. Maker-maker round-trip on a weather pair ≈
   **−0.3¢** (net rebate) vs ≈3.5¢ taker-taker — the fee wall inverts in the exact category
   (weather) with {identity ✓, fast capital ✓, exit window ✓}.
3. `GET /trade-api/v2/series/fee_changes` is live, public, and currently empty — a cheap tripwire
   worth polling for scheduled per-series fee changes before any sizing decision.
