# Full backtest run — 2026-06-11

Owner asked to "run the full backtest." Ran the complete canonical pipeline end-to-end on the freshest
pull. **This is the method-demo pipeline, NOT the 0014 confirmatory run** (frozen until ≥14 event-days;
running it now and quoting it as "the result" is the L19 violation the prereg exists to prevent). Every
figure is **paper/gross** (fees + spread netted; latency, leg-fill, slippage, toxicity NOT) and on
**~1 effective day** of independent signal (see [deployment-readiness-2026-06-11.md](deployment-readiness-2026-06-11.md):
91% of candidate arbs fell on 06-10). Logs: `scripts/_data/bt_*.log`.

**Data:** 48.4 h (2.02 d), 60,492 transitions, 358 markets. 6,146 episodes (5,864 clean, 281
restart-censored, excluded). By category: sports 4,830 / weather 1,284 / econ 32. 36 pre-remap
threshold-econ records quarantined (0013); 621 pre-0013 CLOSEs lag-corrected −1.25 s.

## Stage results

**1. `analyze_persistence` — edge + persistence.** Median edge **0.39¢**, p75 0.75¢, p90 1.21¢, max
42.63¢. Counts/day: ≥1¢ 428, ≥2¢ 186, ≥5¢ 55. Persistence: median **3 s**, only **19% last ≥30 s**,
1% ≥300 s. Capturable (net>0, c2≥1, no window floor): 4,073 — but the **robust cohort (≥1¢, ≥30 s,
c2≥10, fresh) is just 35 episodes** over 2 days. The fat tail is phantoms: the 7 fattest episodes are
`sfohigh-lt82f` 42¢ @1 s (flicker), `mlb-cin-sd` 37¢ @4 s, and five ITF/valorant with **open_net
0.0–1.0¢ but 36–37¢ "peak"** (book-init/WIDEN spikes) — the exact L20 signatures.

**2. `capital_sim` — capacity frontier.** Peak concurrent capital **$31k** → **$101/day** → **0.3%/day**
on peak. Frontier saturates with clip size ($50 clip → 0.4%/day; uncapped → 0.2%/day). Intraday arrivals
peak **13–20 Z (≈9 am–4 pm ET)**, NOT an evening cluster — the tentative evening-cluster hypothesis is
**not supported** by this window.

**3. `account_sim` — the practical $500 number.** 292 capturable arbs but only **3 enter** (the first
deep clips consume the bankroll); **1 realized / 2 locked**. Realized PnL **+$1.97 (+0.39%)**, ending
+0.86% mark-to-edge, over 2 days. ~67% of entered capital sits locked. This is the honest "what a $500
bot actually does this window" — small, and most capital freezes.

**4. `alloc_policy_experiment` — ordering.** $500: FIFO $2.36, **reservation (τ=2.47¢) $28.36
(+1104%)**, offline-knapsack $24.63. `batch1s` = FIFO (**+0.0%** — the "wait 1 s + sort" idea buys
nothing). **τ here is in-sample oracle-fit — not a deployable magnitude ([L19]).**

**5. `clip_threshold_test` — the OOS test (the real backtest).**
- In-sample τ×clip surface peaks at τ=2¢ (+991% vs FIFO) — oracle ceiling, not deployable.
- **Out-of-sample (causal arrival split, fixed rule applied to the unseen late half):** the
  **non-oracle deployable rule (τ=2¢ + 5%-bankroll clip cap) = +197% vs FIFO**; the FITTED (τ*,clip*
  from train) = +515% (oracle-tainted); **clip-cap ALONE = −12%** (risk-control, not PnL).
- **Friction sensitivity:** FIFO → **$0 at ≥1¢ haircut**, while the threshold design stays positive
  ($3.73 at 2¢ haircut). The threshold's job is to skip thin arbs that friction zeroes out.

## Honest read (consistent with the readiness audit)

The pipeline runs end-to-end and produces a **real directional signal**: a 2¢ edge floor + small clip
cap beats FIFO out-of-sample and survives friction that kills FIFO, and weather settlement is clean. But:

- **The magnitudes are preliminary method-demo, not validated.** +197% OOS / +1104% reservation are on
  ~1 effective day, dominated by a few positions, with τ partly oracle-fit. Do not quote them as edge.
- **The fat tail is phantoms** (71% per audit A); the robust tradeable cohort is **35 episodes / 2 days**.
- **The practical return is tiny and capital-bound:** +0.39% realized / 2 days at $500, ~0.3%/day at the
  capacity frontier, most capital locking — *before* the frictions the audit showed land in the bad
  direction (17–29% naked legs, unmeasured unwind cost, fat-edge toxicity).
- All **paper/gross**. Realized would be lower.

**Verdict unchanged:** the backtest confirms a structurally-sound pipeline and a small directional edge,
not a deployable, validated, consistently-profitable strategy. It re-runs in one command
(`bt_*.log`) and should be re-read on the multi-week post-0013 data the 0014 protocol requires.
